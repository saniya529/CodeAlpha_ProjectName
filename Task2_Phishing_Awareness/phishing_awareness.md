# Phishing Awareness Training

## 1. Introduction
Phishing is an online scam in which criminals try to trick people into sharing personal or financial information.

## 2. Common Types of Phishing
- Email Phishing
- SMS Phishing (Smishing)
- Website Phishing
- Social Media Phishing

## 3. Warning Signs
- Unknown sender
- Suspicious links
- Spelling mistakes
- Urgent messages
- Requests for passwords or OTPs
- Unexpected attachments

## 4. How to Stay Safe
- Never share your OTP or password.
- Check the sender's email address.
- Avoid clicking unknown links.
- Use strong passwords.
- Enable two-factor authentication.
- Report suspicious messages.

## 5. Example
A message says: "Your bank account will be blocked today. Click this unknown link immediately."

This is suspicious because it creates urgency and asks the user to click an unknown link.

## 6. Conclusion
Phishing awareness helps people identify online scams and protect their personal information.
