PHISHING AWARENESS TRAINING



1\. Introduction

Phishing is an online scam in which criminals trick people into sharing personal or financial information.



2\. Types of Phishing

\- Email Phishing

\- SMS Phishing (Smishing)

\- Website Phishing

\- Social Media Phishing



3\. Warning Signs

\- Unknown sender

\- Suspicious links

\- Spelling mistakes

\- Urgent messages

\- Requests for passwords or OTPs

\- Unexpected attachments



4\. Social Engineering

Social engineering is a technique used to manipulate people into sharing confidential information or taking unsafe actions.



5\. Real-World Examples

\- A fake bank message asks you to update account details.

\- A fake delivery message asks you to click a link to pay a fee.

\- A fake support agent asks for your OTP.

\- An email pretends to be from a company and asks you to open an attachment.



6\. How to Stay Safe

\- Never share your OTP or password.

\- Check the sender carefully.

\- Avoid unknown links.

\- Use strong passwords.

\- Enable two-factor authentication.

\- Report suspicious messages.

\- Verify requests through official channels.
